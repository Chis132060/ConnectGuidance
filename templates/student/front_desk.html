University Guidance Front Desk

Information for in-person consultations, walk-in services, and emergency crisis response.

Operating Hours & Location

Main University Campus

Office Schedule

Monday – Friday: 08:00 AM – 05:00 PM

Closed on Weekends and University Holidays

Walk-In Procedures

  1. Check-In at Reception Desk: Present your valid Student ID card to the attending staff member.
  2. Intake Triage: The receptionist will record your primary concern (academic, personal, emotional, or career) and check real-time counselor availability.
  3. Assignment: If an opening exists, you will be booked into the earliest available slot. If fully booked, a scheduled session for a future date will be reserved.

24/7 Immediate Crisis Support

If you or someone you know is experiencing acute emotional crisis, self-harm thoughts, or overwhelming distress, immediate confidential help is available 24/7.

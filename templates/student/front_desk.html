Walk-In Procedures

Steps to access guidance services in person

1

Check-In at Reception Desk

Present your valid Student ID card to the attending staff member upon arrival.

2

Intake Triage

The receptionist will record your primary concern — academic, personal, emotional, or career — and check real-time counselor availability.

3

Assignment

If an opening exists, you will be booked into the earliest available slot. If fully booked, a scheduled session for a future date will be reserved.

24/7 Immediate Crisis Support

If you or someone you know is experiencing acute emotional crisis, self-harm thoughts, or overwhelming distress, immediate confidential help is available 24/7. You are not alone.
